Right-Sizing Rule
PV kWp ≈ monthly kWh ÷ 120. A typical 500 kWh/month home needs ~4.2 kWp, not a forced 5 kWp. Oversizing increases export, which is compensated at a lower rate, reducing overall ROI. The donuts above show approximate fill level relative to the 10 kWp domestic cap.

Practical note: Choose hybrid-ready inverter on Day 1
Even if you don't install a battery immediately, selecting a battery-ready / hybrid-compatible inverter architecture now preserves the Year 10 retrofit option cleanly. A messy retrofit with an incompatible inverter can cost RM3,000–6,000 extra and erode the economics.
Analyst Conclusion
Solar ATAP makes sense for the right household. Battery timing is the key decision.
The financial case depends on usage level and understanding the two-phase structure. The question is not just "should I buy solar?" — it's "when should I add a battery, and what size system should I build toward?"
Day 1 Decisions
Right-size PV — kWh ÷ 120 = kWp. Do not oversize.

Battery optional — only clearly justified for 800+ kWh/month.

Hybrid-ready inverter — always, regardless of current battery decision.
Year 10 Decision
Reassess battery — export value drops to zero, making battery economics materially better.

Battery prices likely lower by 2035–36. Wait, then act.

High and Very High — retrofit strongly justified. Medium — evaluate case-by-case.
